FAQ

Straight answers.

Talk to a roofer, not a call center. (512) 555-0187

Mon–Sat, 7 a.m. to 7 p.m. The storm line answers 24/7.

Will insurance pay for a new roof after hail?

If a covered storm damaged it, usually yes, minus your deductible. We'll inspect for free and tell you honestly whether the damage is worth a claim. Sometimes it isn't, and we'll say so.

Can you file or negotiate my claim for me?

No. Texas doesn't allow a contractor to act as a public adjuster on a job they're doing, and it shouldn't. We document the damage, meet your adjuster on the roof and provide a detailed scope. You file the claim and you own it.

Can you cover my deductible?

No. Waiving, rebating or absorbing a deductible is illegal in Texas, and contracts tied to an insurance claim must say that you pay it. A roofer who offers is a red flag.

How long does a replacement take?

Most homes take one day. Steep, very large, metal and tile roofs take two to four days.

Do Class 4 shingles really lower my premium?

Many Texas insurers offer a discount for UL 2218 Class 4 roofs. Ask your agent for the number; we'll send them the certificate they need after install.

What if it rains during the install?

We only tear off what we can dry in the same day, and every crew carries tarps. Our project managers start watching radar at 5 a.m.

Do you handle permits and HOA approval?

Yes. We pull permits where your city requires them and prepare HOA color and material submittals.
